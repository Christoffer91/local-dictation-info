Pilot privacy and support

Effective 20 October 2026. Christoffer handles support personally and clears closed routine cases within 30 days.

Privacy in Clear Dictation

Clear Dictation is the English store name of Local Dictation; both names refer to the same app published by Christoffer Hansen.

Who provides the app?

Christoffer Hansen provides Local Dictation and is the selected personal operator. The app has no active managed transcription service, app account or subscription. Dictation does not pass through a server run by the app publisher. You arrange and manage any cloud provider accounts yourself.

For app or privacy questions, contact Christoffer Hansen at christofferhansen2@gmail.com. He handles requests personally. The email link opens only when you select it; the app does not automatically attach audio, history or API keys. You choose what to send through your email service. The handling of requests is described below.

Local processing and model downloads

Local Whisper is the default engine. It processes audio on your device with the model you select. The four model options are NB-Whisper base, NB-Whisper small, Whisper multilingual base and Whisper multilingual small. You start model downloads yourself. Downloads retrieve model files from Hugging Face and its file delivery infrastructure; they do not upload recordings, transcripts or your vocabulary. The download service receives network information needed for the request and may keep its own logs. See Hugging Face privacy information.

iOS can continue an existing model download when you switch apps. This is separate from recording and transcription. Selecting a model starts no download. A fresh installation must download a model before local Whisper can be used; it is not ready for offline use without that preparation.

For local Apple recognition, the app requires on-device support and explicitly requests on-device processing. It checks availability and speech permission for the selected language. It does not switch to a cloud engine if local recognition is unavailable. iOS may manage its own speech resources and system data; this does not promise that every OS operation is offline.

You select Norwegian or English for dictation independently of the interface language. Auto is available only when the selected engine and the app's capability checks allow it. Local Auto remains disabled in the version described here.

When you choose cloud transcription

Azure Speech MAI-2, Azure OpenAI, OpenAI and OpenRouter are optional engines requiring your own provider account, API key and correct configuration. Azure uses the resource/deployment you supply; direct OpenAI and OpenRouter use their validated provider addresses. Selecting an engine or saving its settings does not upload audio. Each cloud attempt requires confirmation for that specific upload. A manual retry requires new confirmation and may incur another charge. Cancellation may arrive after the provider has processed or charged for the attempt. The app does not retry automatically or silently switch providers.

After confirmation, the app sends the audio file, selected model/deployment, any language selection and necessary authentication to the service. Depending on the engine, vocabulary, keywords or a prompt may also be sent as recognition context. The OpenRouter option does not send the app's vocabulary or prompt. Replacement rules are applied locally to the result. The transcription request does not include your whole history or local filenames; the audio has a fixed upload filename. Audio and context you supply may nevertheless contain personal information.

The recipient and its service providers may process content and request information under their own terms. Account, security, usage and billing records need separate consideration from audio content. Bringing your own key does not establish zero retention, no training or processing only within the EU/EEA. The relevant primary sources are Azure Speech, Azure OpenAI, OpenAI API and OpenRouter.

OpenRouter is an intermediary that forwards audio to an underlying model provider. The current app selects a model but does not pin the underlying provider or send an explicit ZDR/data-policy setting. This version does not guarantee a particular processing region, zero retention or exclusion from training across the full route. Check the actual account configuration and agreements before using confidential material.

Recording and transcription when switching apps

Background recording is unavailable in this pilot's ordinary Release build. Keep the app open while recording. Entering the background stops the microphone and retains the saved portion for further handling. Returning to the app does not restart the microphone. A previously saved background-recording preference cannot activate the capability in this version.

Continue transcription in the background is a separate option, off by default. When enabled, local Whisper processing and an already confirmed and started cloud attempt may continue if iOS grants runtime. A local job can process the saved portion after the microphone stops. This does not start a cold microphone or grant unlimited background execution. New cloud recordings are never automatically uploaded from the background; they require confirmation in the app. Local Apple recognition requires the app to remain open. If interrupted, audio is retained for a manual retry under the app's cleanup policy.

Background transcription does not change the clipboard while the app is inactive. Keyboard sharing can publish completed saved text if you enabled it; device locking may prevent that handoff without removing the saved text.

What stays on your device?

History stores transcripts, dates and language and engine/model metadata until you delete it. Vocabulary, replacement rules, language choices and other preferences are stored locally. Service addresses and model/deployment details are saved as cloud settings. API keys are stored separately in this device's Keychain with synchronization disabled, and are sent only to authenticate to the validated provider address. A saved key is not filled back into the editing field.

Audio is deleted after successful text storage by default. Keep audio for playback is a separate option, off by default. When enabled, a local copy can remain available until 24 hours after the original recording or import. Retrying transcription does not extend that deadline. Playing audio requires a separate tap. Unfinished or failed recordings are kept for recovery under the app's separate 24-hour cleanup policy.

Expired audio must not be played or uploaded. Physical deletion takes place when app cleanup can run; iOS suspension or storage failures may delay it. You can delete remaining audio from the recording list. Turning playback retention off makes the app remove saved playback audio and references before confirming the change. Errors are shown while successfully saved text is preserved.

The app uses local file protection and excludes its storage areas from backups. It has no app-provided iCloud synchronization for recordings, models or history. These are implemented controls, not a promise of secure erasure of every OS copy or independently verified protection on every device.

Copying, sharing and the keyboard

Copy and Share use text. History export previews the selected text and dates in UTC before opening the share sheet; it excludes audio, keys and cloud configuration.

Automatic copying is off by default. When enabled, successfully saved text is copied to the clipboard while the app is active. If transcription finishes in the background, you must open the app and choose Copy yourself. Manual Copy works independently of the setting. The app marks its clipboard writes as local to this device, with a two-minute expiration.

Keyboard sharing is a separate option, also off by default. When enabled, the latest completed text, a random document identifier and language/expiry metadata can be stored in a local App Group for two minutes. The keyboard checks expiration when you choose Insert. It has no microphone, does not request Full Access and does not read surrounding text. You switch between apps yourself. Turning sharing off revokes the app's shared copy; physical cleanup depends on the app lifecycle and any errors.

Local Dictation cannot revoke text already pasted, inserted, exported or sent to another app or recipient. Those recipients handle it under their own policies.

Apple TestFlight and feedback

TestFlight is a separate data flow. Apple automatically collects crash and usage information and shares it with the publisher. It can include device/OS details and your name/email depending on the invitation. You cannot disable this within TestFlight. See TestFlight and Privacy.

Feedback you choose to send can include comments, screenshots and technical details. Apple's guide describes this separate channel. Use invented test content only; do not attach dictation, history, keys or screenshots containing private information. Apple states that beta feedback is retained for one year, while crash/usage data may remain until bugs are resolved. This is separate from the publisher's 30-day routine for ordinary support cases below.

The described source has no publisher-operated advertising, tracking or crash-upload SDK. That does not remove processing by Apple or the network services you choose.

Support and publisher handling

Christoffer Hansen is the controller for information he uses for limited pilot support and defect correction. He uses voluntary support messages and necessary Apple diagnostic information only to answer questions, investigate defects and improve this pilot. The need is a short expected/actual description, app version, device/iOS and selected language/engine. The sender's address and normal email metadata accompany a request. Dictation, history, API keys, provider addresses and attachments are not requested. He does not use support information for marketing or model training, or place it in public issues. TestFlight information is not shared onward with third parties.

Closed, routine support cases and necessary local case notes are deleted or genuinely anonymized within 30 days after the case is closed, under a manual routine that Christoffer handles personally. Anonymized defect descriptions must no longer identify you. The deadline covers case information he controls; it does not override Apple's retention, the email provider's system/security data, or necessary records for a specific legal obligation or dispute. Any such exception is justified, limited and given its own retention assessment. Unsolicited private content is minimized and deleted as soon as appropriate, and is not used as ordinary test material.

The legal basis for this limited support and necessary defect correction is legitimate interests under GDPR Article 6(1)(f): answering requests and making the pilot safer and more reliable. The assessment relies on minimal content, personal follow-up, short retention and the fact that private dictation is unnecessary for this purpose. You can object by contacting the address above. Legal obligations relating to rights requests are assessed separately. Voluntary submission or the app's upload button is not automatically treated as legal consent. See the Norwegian Data Protection Authority's guidance.

The support address uses Gmail. Google handles messages and service information under its privacy policy and data-transfer information. Processing may take place outside Norway/the EEA. The publisher's deletion routine does not guarantee immediate removal of all Google's operational or backup copies. This notice promises no particular account configuration, EU storage or specific data-processing agreement.

Privacy and support pages

The information pages are prepared for static publication on GitHub Pages in a separate public repository, with Norwegian/English privacy and support pages and review instructions. The pages have no login, contact form, publisher analytics or external fonts. They offer no audio download. When you visit GitHub Pages, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security. Its handling and retention follow its privacy statement; no particular region or fixed log-retention period is promised here.

Your choices and rights

You can deny microphone/speech permission in iOS, choose a local engine, decline an upload, and turn off automatic copying, keyboard sharing, background transcription and playback retention. You can also delete audio or history, remove models and delete API keys in cloud settings. Deleting a key in the app does not close the provider account or remove its logs; revoke the key with the provider when needed. Do not rely on uninstalling the app to revoke a key.

The app does not send local dictation history to a server operated by the publisher, and the publisher cannot retrieve history from your device. For information the publisher actually handles, contact the address above to request access, correction, deletion or another applicable right. Do not send identity documents or dictation with the initial request. Only necessary information for safe identity verification should be requested. Requests are assessed under applicable rules and deadlines. Depending on the processing, you may also have rights of restriction, portability and objection, and to withdraw valid privacy consent. You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority. See its overview of data protection rights.

Upload permission is a technical control. It does not by itself establish a legal basis for processing other people's information, employer data or sensitive recordings. Use such material only after you and the relevant responsible organization have established that the processing is permitted.
